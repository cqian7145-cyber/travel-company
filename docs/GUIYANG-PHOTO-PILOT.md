# Guiyang photo pilot

Guiyang is available at `/cities/guiyang` and in the city directory. It is a photo-led first look built from seven supplied images, shown in this order:

1. 太平路 — Taiping Road
2. 国家生态会议中心 — National Ecological Conference Center
3. 白宫 — Guiyang White House
4. 甲秀楼 — Jiaxiu Tower, night
5. 海豚广场 — Dolphin Square
6. 黔灵山公园 — Qianling Mountain Park
7. 花溪公园 — Huaxi Park

The labels follow the contributor's mapping. The exact photo locations, access and entrances, opening hours, admission, reservations, transit, and image-use rights have not been independently verified. Amap links perform name-based area searches; they do not confirm the result or provide venue directions.

Stories, Experience Journeys, field-verified place records, and a Guiyang-specific itinerary are not part of this pilot. The generated offline manifest records the Guiyang content version, and the supplied source images remain unchanged outside the repository.

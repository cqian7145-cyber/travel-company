# Connectivity, Speak Local, First 90 Minutes

Extend the existing local Shanghai companion. Preserve current work and the paper/olive/burgundy design. No new dependencies, paid calls, ticket sales, live airport navigation, live traffic, or telecom sales.

Connectivity separates mobile internet, home-number SMS and access to particular services. Preparation depends on device family, exact model/sales region/carrier, connection plan and SMS needs. Four independent symptom guides include concrete Pudong passport Wi-Fi recovery and dated official references. Unknown compatibility and counter hours remain unknown.

Speak Local provides 24 fixed Chinese/English scenarios across taxi, dining, station, hotel, shopping and directions. Local hotel/contact/train details and itinerary destinations fill templates. Missing or unconfirmed full addresses block destination cards. Dietary/allergy cards explicitly discuss ingredients, sauces, utensils and cross-contact, with no food-safety guarantee or claim of clinical/professional certification. Copy and full-viewport Chinese display; no generative translation or speech service.

First 90 Minutes takes PVG/SHA, terminal, arrival period, luggage and hotel/address. Generates a five-step action card: connect, payment backup, transport choices, correct pickup, hotel check-in. Reference costs are tied to their actual reference destination, not extrapolated to arbitrary hotels. Rail/maglev/bus operating hours are not live-verified; late-night arrivals get a taxi fallback and explicit service checks. Source dates differ from retrieval dates.

Offline: explicit user-triggered service-worker installation caches only versioned local app assets. No API, passport, payment, remote image or third-party font cache. Offline readiness is checked against actual cache entries, not an optimistic local flag. Downloadable standalone HTML backup contains the four guides, phrases and chosen arrival details without remote assets or scripts. Personal details stay browser-local. Browser storage can be evicted; advise saving/testing before flight. Validate offline reload and backup-file use in a real browser.

Success evidence here is functional verification, not tourist comprehension or hotel demand. Field validation remains: can staff understand the basic request without another translation app; does the arrival card reduce guest questions? No analytics or personal-event transmission added.
